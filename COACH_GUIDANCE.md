# Coach Guidance

This workshop exists to give learners some practice **working with classes without having to define them**.

## Delivery

1. Explain the learning objectives
2. Work through the background around why classes are useful (do some live coding if that's your thing)
3. Explain the first exercise, give learners 10 mins to do it then allow time for reflection / questions
4. Explain the second exercise, give learners 15 mins to do it then allow time for reflection / questions

> You may feel demos of exercise 1 and 2 are needed. If so, you can either do them yourself or ask for a volunteer :)
